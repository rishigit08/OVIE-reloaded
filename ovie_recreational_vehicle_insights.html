Policy details

What's included9
Liability coverage for bodily injury and property damage an insured is legally responsible for from an accident involving a covered RV, with defense costs paid in addition to the limit
Named insured, family members, and permissive users are covered as an “insured” under liability coverage
Uninsured/Underinsured Motorist coverage (bodily injury and property damage) and Medical Payments, when a premium is shown on the Declarations
Collision coverage for loss from collision or overturn, when shown on the Declarations
Comprehensive coverage for loss from perils other than collision — theft, vandalism, fire, flood, animal impact, hail — when shown on the Declarations
Permanently attached customized equipment is covered, subject to policy terms
Emergency Expense coverage for lodging, transportation, meals, and pet boarding if the RV is inoperable or uninhabitable more than 50 miles from principal storage
Pet Protection for veterinary costs, boarding, recovery, and equipment replacement
Towing and Labor coverage, when a premium is shown, limited to 6 occurrences per policy period
What's not included5
Commercial, rental, livery, or business use, unless business or artisan use is specifically stated on the Declarations
Use as a permanent residence, and premises liability arising from that use
Wear and tear, mechanical or electrical breakdown, rust, rot, corrosion, mold, infestation, water leaks or damage, and gradual deterioration
Portable equipment, personal effects, electronics, and other items not permanently installed, unless separately endorsed
Racing, intentional acts, use by an unlicensed driver, and unauthorized use
Coverage limitations8
Physical-damage deductibles are not stated in this form and would be shown on the Declarations Page
Uninsured Motorist Property Damage pays only in excess of a $250 deductible
Emergency Expense is capped at $250 per day, up to the maximum shown on the Declarations, and applies only more than 50 miles from principal storage
Pet Protection is limited to $1,000 per incident and $3,000 per policy period, plus separate sub-limits for boarding, recovery, and equipment
Towing and Labor is limited to 6 occurrences per policy period
A newly acquired or replacement RV must be reported within 30 calendar days for automatic coverage to continue
Permissive-user limitations, operator licensing, and garaging duties apply
Theft or vandalism must be reported within 24 hours, or as soon as practicable

Moderate

Policy specifications

Newly acquired or replacement RV

Must be reported to the company within 30 calendar days of acquisition for automatic coverage to continue; otherwise, no ongoing coverage applies.

Policy territory

United States, its territories or possessions, and Canada, including while the RV is being transported between ports.

Valuation and loss settlement

Payment is capped at the lowest of actual cash value, the cost to repair or replace, or a stated amount if declared; depreciation and betterment adjustments apply.

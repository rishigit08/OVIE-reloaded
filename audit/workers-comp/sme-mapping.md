## Common interaction rules

Native independent disclosures, modal focus trapping and restoration, source links to the original PDF, global search revealing matching parents, and schedule filters. Source sheets use stacked labeled facts for tables, explicit document names/PDF pages and separate form references; composite facts expose every supporting source. The standard source surface is a mobile bottom sheet and desktop right drawer. Keyboard interactions are immediate and reduced motion removes movement. The WC SME changes content and ordering, not global typography or the BOP-specific centered source treatment.

## Prototype boundaries

Ask Ovie remains a local sample-answer prototype. Sharing, voice input, and feedback submission do not connect to services. The design-review pass covers the current Insights contract changes; it does not implement a backend or replace policy interpretation by the agent/carrier.

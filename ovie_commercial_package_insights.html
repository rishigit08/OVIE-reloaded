Policy details

What’s included5

Commercial Property

Blanket building & business personal property limit
$4,831,700

Shared across all 3 scheduled buildings; excludes stock from this blanket. Each building refers to the same limit.

Location-specific conditions

$5,000 property deductible at each location. Building and business personal property show replacement cost / agreed value; earthquake coverage has a separate 10% deductible.

General Liability

Occurrence-based liability

Covers qualifying bodily injury and property damage occurring during the policy period. Premises and operations are described in the location and classification schedules.

Each occurrence
$1,000,000
General aggregate
$2,000,000

A separate location aggregate applies to qualifying losses attributable only to one designated location.

Products-completed operations aggregate
$2,000,000

Shown in the declarations. The class schedule assigns products-completed operations to the general aggregate; confirm which aggregate applies to a specific claim.

Personal & advertising injury
$1,000,000

Any one person or organization.

Other liability benefits

Medical expense: $10,000 per person. Damage to rented premises: $300,000 per premises, subject to the endorsed causes and conditions.

Employee benefits liability · claims-made

$1,000,000 each employee / $2,000,000 aggregate; $1,000 deductible per employee. Retroactive date: October 30, 2017. Covers negligent benefits administration, subject to claim timing.

Inland Marine

Equipment conditions

Excess Liability

Each occurrence
$3,000,000

Aggregate

Except covered autos and products-completed operations; a separate $3,000,000 products-completed operations aggregate is shown.

How the excess part applies

Follows the applicable underlying coverage, subject to its own terms, and provides no broader coverage. Personal and advertising injury: $3,000,000 per person or organization. Self-insured retention: none shown.

Referenced companion policies

Union Insurance Company Auto and Employers Liability policies appear in the underlying schedule. Their full policy documents are not included here.

Business income

Business income & extra expense
Actual loss sustained

Within the period of restoration, up to 12 consecutive months after covered physical loss or damage. No fixed dollar limit is shown for this main coverage.

Covered-loss trigger

A necessary suspension of operations must result from covered direct physical loss or damage at described premises. A fall in revenue alone does not establish this trigger.

Waiting period

Business income begins after 72 hours. Extra expense begins immediately after covered physical loss or damage. Specific extensions have their own conditions.

Policy specifications5

Property and income enhancements

Premier Choice endorsements add or change property and income extensions, each with its own conditions and limits.

Location aggregate endorsement

Creates a separate general aggregate for qualifying claims attributable only to operations at a single designated location.

Broader rented-premises causes

The liability enhancement adds specified causes including lightning, explosion, smoke and fire-protection leakage to rented-premises damage protection.

Employee benefits endorsement

Adds claims-made coverage for negligent benefits administration. It does not turn this package into EPLI.

Endorsement summary

Highlights from the supplied policy

The highlights above summarize material effects. The source schedules retain the full forms and notices inventory for each part.

Sub-limits2

Property enhancement schedule

A large schedule sets separate caps for property extensions, including records, electronic equipment, money, theft, spoilage and property away from the premises. Review the schedule for a specific loss type.

Income extension schedule

Civil authority, extended income, dependent properties, food contamination, utility interruption and other extensions have separate durations, limits or conditions.

What’s not included5

Commercial Property · wear and tear

Wear and tear and deterioration are excluded; specified resulting losses remain subject to the form’s exceptions.

General Liability · workplace practices

Specified injury from employment practices, including harassment and discrimination, is excluded. This is separate from Employers Liability for employee injury.

General Liability · data-related liability

Specified disclosure of confidential information and electronic-data damages are excluded, with a limited bodily-injury exception.

Inland Marine · equipment restrictions

The equipment form excludes highway vehicles and property leased, rented or loaned to others, among other restrictions.

Excess Liability · abuse or molestation

The endorsement excludes injury or damage arising from specified abuse or molestation and related negligent supervision or employment.

Coverage limitations5

Property deductibles

$5,000 is shown at each location; building and business personal property have a separate 10% earthquake deductible.

Equipment and benefits deductibles

Equipment: $1,000. Employee benefits liability: $1,000 each employee. These apply to their own coverage, not to the package as a whole.

Business income timing

The main income coverage has a 72-hour wait and maximum 12-month duration. Extra expense starts immediately; specific extensions use their own timing.

Claims-made benefits coverage

The negligent act must fall on or after October 30, 2017 and within the policy period; the claim must meet the endorsement’s first-made and reporting provisions.

Maintain controlling underlying insurance

The excess part requires its controlling underlying insurance to be maintained. Failure to maintain it does not make the excess part fill that missing layer.

Coverage watch points4
Moderate
One loss can touch several parts

Property, liability and equipment use separate limits and deductibles. A loss involving more than one part must meet each part’s conditions; there is no combined package limit.

Moderate
Check the locations involved

Property coverages are assigned to the declared locations. Newly acquired and off-premises extensions are limited, so check their conditions before assuming another site is covered.

Moderate
Companion documents are referenced

The excess schedule references Auto and Employers Liability policies. Upload those documents, if available, to review their own terms alongside this package. Their absence here does not establish missing insurance.

Upload companion policy
Low
Income and expense start at different times

The first 72 hours are outside the main business-income restoration period. Extra expense begins immediately after covered physical loss or damage.

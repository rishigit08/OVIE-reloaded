Policy details

What’s included7

Liability above underlying limits

Pays covered bodily injury, property damage, personal injury and advertising injury damages above the applicable retained limit. For the excess layer, underlying limits respond first; Allianz’s defense duty starts when all applicable scheduled limits are exhausted by covered damages.

Drop-down coverage

When this umbrella covers damages that no scheduled underlying policy covers, its defense duty can apply and damages are subject to the $25,000 per-occurrence SIR. This is conditional broader coverage, not a promise to fill every gap.

Employers liability damages and settlements

Scheduled employers liability can receive excess protection for covered damages, including settlements. Workers compensation medical and wage benefits under statutory obligations are excluded.

Employee benefits liability

Claims-made following-form coverage, subject to the scheduled retroactive date and claim timing. It cannot provide broader coverage than scheduled underlying insurance.

Opticians professional liability

Scheduled professional liability may receive excess coverage. The professional-services exclusion’s exception is limited to coverage provided by a scheduled policy.

Covered events worldwide

Covered occurrences and business-related personal or advertising injury offenses can take place anywhere in the world, subject to the policy period, knowledge provisions and exclusions.

Defense subject to the policy limit

Covered investigation, defense, settlement and appeal costs reduce the applicable limit under the settlement-costs endorsement.

What’s not included19

Workers compensation benefits

Statutory workers compensation, disability and unemployment obligations are excluded, including required medical and wage benefits.

Asbestos

Asbestos-related liability and the specified defense and indemnity obligations are excluded.

Electronic data and hosted chatrooms

Electronic-data loss, corruption and access/manipulation damages are excluded, along with specified hosted chatroom or bulletin-board injury.

Professional services outside the exception

Professional-services claims are excluded unless a scheduled policy covers them; the umbrella is then no broader.

Underlying exclusions for follow-form coverage

Employee benefits and professional liability follow the applicable scheduled coverage; an underlying exclusion is not removed by this umbrella.

Fungi or bacteria

Specified building-related injury and remediation costs are excluded; the bodily-injury/property-damage exclusion has a consumption-product exception.

Silica or silica-related dust

Silica-related injury, damage and response costs are excluded.

Tobacco and tobacco products

Tobacco-related liability, injury and exposure are excluded.

Managed care operations

Liability arising out of managed care operations is excluded.

Insurance and related operations

Specified financed-property, fiduciary and insurance-related operations are excluded.

Pollution

Pollution liability and cleanup costs are excluded, with specified hostile-fire, vehicle and products exceptions tied to scheduled coverage.

Employment practices and sexual misconduct

Specified employment-related injuries and sexual-misconduct injury are excluded.

Own property and first-party auto

Specified property in your ownership or control and first-party auto benefits are excluded; property exceptions remain subject to the wording.

Aircraft and watercraft restrictions

Aircraft/watercraft liability has limited exceptions; aircraft products and grounding liability are excluded.

Certain terrorism outside the United States

Other acts of terrorism outside the U.S. are excluded only when the endorsement’s damage, casualty or material-use conditions apply.

War and nuclear risks

War-related injury and damage, and the specified nuclear energy liability risks, are excluded.

ERISA obligations

Obligations under ERISA or similar laws are excluded.

Liquor liability outside scheduled coverage

Specified liquor liability is excluded except to the extent valid scheduled underlying coverage exists or was exhausted.

Communications and intellectual property

Specified unlawful communications, intellectual-property infringement and unauthorized-name use are excluded; the advertising exception is limited.

Coverage limitations9

Follow form has a defined scope

Employee benefits and professional liability cannot broaden the scheduled coverage. Additional insureds also receive coverage no broader than their underlying insurance. This is not a blanket follow-form policy.

Maintain underlying insurance

Keep scheduled coverage in effect, avoid material term changes and preserve scheduled limits, except permitted aggregate erosion. Notify Allianz as soon as practicable of lapse or changed terms.

Lapse does not lower the attachment point

Failure to maintain underlying insurance does not invalidate this policy; it responds only as if the requirements had been met. It does not replace the missing underlying layer.

Self-Insured Retention (SIR): $25,000

Applies separately to each occurrence producing damages not covered by any scheduled underlying insurance. It is not the premium and does not replace scheduled attachment limits.

Scheduled underlying retentions continue

The schedule lists $558,000 each-occurrence retentions, with no aggregate, for CGL, employee benefits and opticians professional liability. The amendment keeps applicable underlying SIRs in force, including after exhaustion.

Defense costs are within limits

The settlement-costs endorsement replaces the base form’s additional-payment treatment. Defense, investigation, settlement and appeal costs erode limits; the defense duty ends when the applicable limit is used up.

Drop-down / Difference in Conditions (DIC)

Broader-than-underlying response follows the insuring, defense and retained-limit provisions. “DIC” describes that behavior here; it is not an express DIC grant or a universal exception to exclusions.

Claims-made employee benefits timing

The act must not precede the scheduled retroactive date or follow the umbrella term. The claim must first be made during the umbrella term or the underlying EBL extended reporting period, if provided.

Aggregate erosion and attachment

Aggregate limits apply separately each annual period. Covered damage payments may reduce or exhaust scheduled limits; the umbrella then applies under Section III.F, subject to its terms. Applicable other insurance also forms part of the retained limit.

Coverage watch points6
Critical
Umbrella is not standalone coverage

Scheduled underlying coverage must be maintained. If it is missing or lapses, this policy pays only what it would have paid had that insurance stayed in force.

Critical
A lower underlying limit can leave a shortfall

If an underlying limit is below its scheduled requirement, the umbrella does not automatically fill the difference. Have the agent verify the schedule requirements; no actual shortfall is established here.

Moderate
Confirm the full schedule across carriers

Several carriers appear in the schedule. Have the agent reconcile all intended underlying policies, especially when multiple brokers are involved. This document does not establish that any policy is unscheduled.

Moderate
Review underlying renewals and any lapse

Entries 1–11 show December 1, 2014 expiry, before the umbrella’s December 31, 2014 end date. Renewals may exist; ask the agent to confirm them and apply the maintenance wording to any lapse.

Moderate
Defense spending uses the coverage limit

Investigation, defense, settlement and appeal expenses can leave less available for damages. The insurer’s defense duty ends when the applicable limit is exhausted.

Low
$25,000 out of pocket for qualifying drop-down losses

Budget for the per-occurrence umbrella SIR when this policy covers damages that scheduled underlying insurance does not cover. Separate scheduled underlying retentions may also apply.

Policy specifications9

Commercial umbrella classification

The forms provide umbrella coverage across multiple commercial coverage types, with conditional drop-down coverage and specific follow-form restrictions.

Scheduled underlying insurance amended

Applicable underlying SIRs remain in force; policies purchased specifically excess of this umbrella are not scheduled underlying insurance.

Employee benefits following form

Adds claims-made treatment and ties employee benefits protection to the scheduled coverage.

Professional liability follow-form

Allows the scheduled professional-liability exception, with no broader umbrella protection for those risks.

Settlement costs reduce limits

Changes both the limit calculations and the point at which the defense duty ends.

Named insureds added

Names Luxottica NYC Inc., One Sight and One Sight Research Foundation alongside Luxottica U.S. Holdings Corp.

Recovery waiver for qualifying contracts

Waives recovery rights where a written pre-occurrence contract requires the waiver and the specified operations or completed-work conditions apply.

Cancellation notice amended

The endorsement specifies 10 days for nonpayment and 60 days for other reasons, amended where necessary for applicable state law.

Certified terrorism cap

The endorsement provides an industry-wide $100 billion Program Year cap mechanism, subject to its conditions. This is not a separate policy limit.

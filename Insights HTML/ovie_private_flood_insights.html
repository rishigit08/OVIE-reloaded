Policy details

What's included5
Direct physical flood damage to the condominium building above NFIP RCBAP maximum limits
Entire condo building, attached additions, fixtures, machinery, equipment and unit improvements
Association-owned personal property in common areas, if contents coverage is elected
Mudflow and certain flood-related erosion are treated as covered flood perils
Losses settle at the lesser of the excess policy limit, actual cash value, or repair or replacement cost

Loss of use, additional living expense, business interruption, and Increased Cost of Compliance
Land, landscaping, fences, decks, pools, hot tubs and spas outside bathroom fixtures

Excess only — pays after the NFIP RCBAP maximum is exhausted, with no separate deductible

Coverage watch points5
Critical

The excess policy only pays after the NFIP RCBAP maximum is reached. If the underlying NFIP RCBAP lapses or is under-limit, this policy may pay nothing.

Moderate

Property in basements or below the lowest elevated floor is only lightly covered — mechanical rooms, storage, and finished basements are largely out of scope.

Moderate

Displacement costs, temporary housing for unit owners, and lost rental income are not covered — those exposures belong on separate policies.

Moderate

Association-owned art, décor and other valuable items in common areas are capped at $2,500 per loss, and antiques settle at functional value.

Low

The provided sample lacks a declarations page, so limits, effective dates and endorsements shown here are illustrative — confirm actual values with your agent.

Policy specifications

Underlying NFIP RCBAP required

An NFIP Residential Condominium Building Association Policy must be in force at maximum primary limits; this excess policy attaches only above that.

Single-building scope

Only one insured building per excess flood policy — additional buildings require separate policies.

Mortgagee and loss payee

Named mortgagees and loss payees are covered for their insurable interest; payments follow the mortgage clause order of precedence.

Endorsements

No endorsements, amendments or riders are included with this sample; all terms reference the base policy form.

Deductibles

No separate deductible

The policy attaches only above the NFIP RCBAP maximum primary limit — that limit acts as the effective deductible.

NFIP RCBAP primary deductibles

Deductibles on the underlying NFIP RCBAP still apply to primary-layer losses and are not covered by this excess policy.

Consumer tip

Keep the underlying NFIP RCBAP in force at maximum primary limits. Ask your agent for proof of the NFIP policy and its deductible schedule so you know where this excess layer attaches.

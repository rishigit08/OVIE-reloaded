How Mosey pieces your insurance together

01 · Scattered

Your coverage lives in a stack of fine print.

A home policy here, an auto renewal there, a life plan from work you've never opened. Each written its own way, by a different insurer.

02 · Read

Mosey reads every page.

Upload, scan or forward your policies. Mosey reads each one end to end and breaks it into pieces: coverages, limits, deductibles and exclusions.

03 · Pieced together

Then fits every piece where it belongs.

Pieces from every policy gather around the people and things you protect. An umbrella policy lands everywhere it applies.

04 · The whole picture

See what's covered. And what isn't there.

When Mosey can't find coverage in your documents, it leaves the space open, so you notice it now instead of after something happens.

05 · Traced

Every piece traces back to its page.

In Mosey, every piece opens to the document and page it came from. No guesses, no assumptions, just what your policy says.

Privacy

On your side of the table.

Mosey states the facts in your documents, with sources, so the decisions stay yours.

  • Doesn't sell insurance

    Mosey doesn't sell policies, recommend insurers or judge whether your coverage is enough.

  • Your documents stay yours

    Your documents are never sold. Nothing is visible to anyone unless you choose to share it.

  • Only what your documents say

    Mosey has no connection to your insurer. Every fact comes from a document you provided.

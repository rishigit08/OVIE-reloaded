Coverage limitations8

Deductible · $5,000 per occurrence

Applies to bodily injury, property damage, and personal/advertising injury damages and defense costs.

Innkeepers’ deductible · $250 per claim

Applies to the guest-property coverage.

Aggregate limits can be used up

Covered payments reduce the applicable aggregate. Additional insureds, locations or projects do not increase it. The remaining balance is not available.

Designated premises

Coverage is limited to the scheduled premises and the premises, projects or operations described by the endorsement.

Contractor certificates

A missing required certificate/additional-insured arrangement can trigger a deductible of $10,000 or 200% of the standard deductible, whichever is greater, for the affected claim.

Swimming pool conditions

If a pool is operated, coverage depends on the endorsement’s enclosure and barrier requirements. A pool is not confirmed in the supplied location description.

Additional insureds · ongoing operations only

Requires a written agreement before loss. Coverage is limited to liability caused by your operations and ends when work is complete.

Premium audit and records

Final premium is subject to audit. The minimum/advance premium is 100%; audit noncompliance can result in a charge of twice that premium.

Guest medical bills

Moderate

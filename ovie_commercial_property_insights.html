What's not included10
Flood damage is not covered.
Electronic-data losses are excluded, with an exception for named perils already covered by this or an underlying policy.
Fungus, rot and bacteria losses are excluded, with limited covered-event exceptions.
Nuclear, biological, chemical and radiological losses are excluded.
Sprinkler leakage caused by earthquake, earth movement or volcanic eruption is excluded.
Asbestos losses are excluded, except direct damage from specified named perils.
Disease-causing virus, bacteria and microorganism losses are excluded.
Terrorism losses are excluded, with a limited state-mandated fire exception.
Pollution losses are excluded except specified-cause exceptions and the limited cleanup extension.
Land and water themselves are not covered property.
Coverage limitations12
Keep underlying insurance in force. It must pay or admit its limits before this layer responds; lower sublimits can prevent recovery.
This policy does not replace an insolvent underlying insurer’s coverage.
Payment is capped by the lowest of adjusted loss, scheduled property value and the policy limit. Applicable deductibles reduce loss and value amounts.
Underlying deductibles apply; amounts by coverage are not stated here.
Protective safeguards follow the primary policy’s conditions; specific alarm, sprinkler and maintenance duties need verification.
Automatic mortgagee / loss-payee status requires a pre-loss written agreement. Submit identities and agreements within 10 business days after the insurer is notified of a loss.
Coverage territory: the 50 U.S. states, their territories and possessions.
Environmental cleanup laws do not extend covered interruption or extra-expense periods.
Trade or economic sanctions can prohibit coverage or claim payment.
Give immediate written notice of an occurrence likely to result in a claim to the contact named in the schedule.
The first named insured handles premiums, policy changes and cancellation. Insurer notices go to that party unless endorsed otherwise.
Appraisal requires both parties’ written agreement. It addresses value or loss amount; the insurer retains its right to deny coverage.

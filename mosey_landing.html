Mosaic

Every policy, organized by who and what it protects.

Pick yourself, your spouse, your home, a vehicle or your business. Mosey shows every coverage from every policy that applies, side by side.

  • Includes policies you own and policies you are covered under, such as a parent's auto plan or an employer's group life.
  • Spans home, auto, liability, business, life, health, disability, travel and pet.
  • Shows where policies overlap, and what is and isn't included.

Ask Mosey. It answers from your policies only.

Ask what a policy says, including right after something goes wrong. Mosey finds the coverages that relate, shows limits, deductibles and exclusions, and lays out what the policy asks you to do and by when.

  • Every answer cites the document and page.
  • Your insurer decides the claim. Mosey helps you understand your policy and know who to call.
A tree fell on my garage. What do I do?
Your homeowners policy lists other structures coverage at $25,000 with a $1,000 deductible. It asks you to give prompt notice and protect the property from further damage.

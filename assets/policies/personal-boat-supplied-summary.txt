## 6. What's Included — Liability and Injury Coverage

- **Watercraft Liability (Part B):** Covers damages for which the insured is legally liable due to ownership, maintenance, or use of a covered vessel, subject to policy exclusions.  
   [Part B, p.5]

- **Medical Payments (Part C):** Pays for medical/funeral expenses for accidental bodily injury to an insured person arising from use of the insured vessel.  
   [Part C, p.6]

- **Uninsured Boater (Part D):** Pays compensatory damages for bodily injury received in a collision with an uninsured vessel, subject to limits.  
   [Part D, p.6]

- **Defense Costs:** Insurer will settle or defend any claim or suit, with defense costs in addition to liability limits (unless otherwise amended by endorsement).  
   [Part B, p.5]

## 7. What's Included — Hull, Equipment, and Watercraft Benefits

- **Property Damage (Part A):** Covers accidental direct physical loss or damage to insured watercraft, spars, sails, machinery, electronics, trailers (if listed), and attached equipment.  
   [Part A: Property Damage, p.3]

- **Towing and Assistance:** Commercial towing and assistance for breakdown or disablement “in the event there is an emergency situation where you and your vessel are not in imminent danger,” up to stated policy limits.  
   [Commercial Towing and Assistance, p.5]

- **Salvage/Wreck Removal:** All sums required to remove the wreck of the insured vessel as required by law, up to policy limits.  
   [Loss Settlement, Part A, p.4]

- **Personal Property Coverage:** Direct and accidental loss to personally owned property on board if insured under Part G and declared on Declarations.  
   [Part G, pp.7–8]

## 11. Valuation and Loss Settlement

- **Physical Damage Valuation:**  
   - Default treatment: policy pays lesser of cost to repair, cost of replacement, or actual cash value—minus depreciation for certain items (see below).
   - For vessel trailer: policy pays the lowest of actual cash value, cost to repair, or cost to replace.  
   [Loss Settlement, Part A, p.4; Part G, p.7]

- **Endorsements (Per Page 13):**
   - **MA7Z04 (Boats >26’):** Loss to plastics/canvas, sails, and outboards/outdrives >5 years old is on ACV/depreciated basis.
   - **MA6508b (Boats <27’):** Same for plastics/canvas, sails, outboards/outdrives >5 years, and machinery inside vessel >7 years old.  
   [Endorsements, p.13]

- **Personal Property/Fishing Equipment:**  
   Pays lowest of ACV, repair, or replacement cost for scheduled items.
   [Part G, p.7]

Coverage limitations8
Property deductible · $10,000

Per occurrence. For multiple buildings at this location, the single largest applicable deductible rule applies, except for earthquake, windstorm or hail.

Optional coverages / glass · $500

As scheduled. The optional coverage deductible applies to the coverages identified in the form.

Liability deductibles · None

The declarations state no bodily injury or property damage liability deductible.

Business income & extra expense · No monetary deductible

Business income still has a 72-hour waiting period; extra expense begins immediately.

Protective safeguards · All four buildings

Maintain working P-1 sprinkler systems and notify the insurer of known impairment. Certain temporary shutdowns have a 48-hour restoration exception. Failure to meet the conditions can exclude fire loss.

Sump and drain maintenance

Water-backup coverage does not apply to losses resulting from failure to maintain pumps or keep drains clear.

Vacancy · More than 60 consecutive days

Specified losses become excluded and other covered loss payments are reduced by 15%. The form’s vacancy definition applies.

Replacement-cost condition · 80%

The limit must be at least 80% of full replacement cost for the form’s full replacement-cost settlement basis. Below that, the form applies a cash-value or proportional calculation, subject to limits.

Coverage watch points4
Moderate
Income starts after 72 hours

A covered interruption can leave the first three days of lost business income outside this coverage. Extra expense begins immediately.

Moderate
Business changes at renewal

Growth or a change in operations may affect BOP eligibility. Ask your agent whether the size and classification of your business still qualify. Current renewal eligibility is not established by this policy.

Moderate
Claims reduce the liability aggregate

Paid claims reduce the amount left in the applicable annual aggregate. These documents do not show the remaining balance.

Low
Seasonal BPP increase is conditional

The 25% seasonal increase requires the BPP limit to be at least 100% of average monthly values for the preceding 12 months, or the shorter time in business.

Policy details

What's included5
Personal property at your rented address, and worldwide, subject to limits and sublimits
Increased living expenses if your rental is uninhabitable, up to 12 months
Family liability for bodily injury or property damage you cause, including legal defense
Guest medical payments for accidental injuries at your rental or from your activities
Personal property settles at replacement cost, not depreciated value
What's not included4
Flood, sewer or drain backup, and earthquake damage to your belongings
Mold, fungus, wet rot, dry rot, or bacteria damage, beyond the limited remediation benefit
Intentional or criminal acts, business use, and lease-cancellation costs
Injuries to you or a resident household member, under liability and guest medical
Coverage limitations4
Sublimits apply: $200 money, $1,000 jewelry, $2,000 firearms, $5,000 electronics, $10,000 rugs
Mold, fungus, and rot remediation is capped at $5,000, only from a covered water loss
Loss-of-use payments run only for the shortest reasonable time, up to 12 months
Guest medical applies only to non-resident guests, not household members
Coverage watch points6
Moderate

Your landlord's building policy does not cover your personal belongings — that's what this renters policy is for.

Moderate

Jewelry, electronics, and other valuables are subject to category sublimits well below your overall Coverage C limit.

Moderate

This policy settles personal property at replacement cost, not actual cash value — confirm that applies to the items that matter most to you.

Moderate

If your lease requires a certificate of insurance or lists an interested party, contact your carrier or agent directly — Ovie cannot issue certificates.

Low

Low
